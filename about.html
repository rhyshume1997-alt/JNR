How we work

From enquiry
to machining

When you phone, you speak to someone who works on the machines rather than a sales desk.

The person pricing your job knows how long it takes to make, so the lead time you're given is one we can hit.

Every part is measured against your drawing before it leaves the workshop, and if your job needs inspection paperwork, it comes with the parts.

Our story

Family run in
Hamilton since 2006

J.N.R. is a family run engineering firm in Hamilton. It started in 2006, and between them the team has over 100 years on the tools.

Our sister company, Marpet Fabrications, is run by the same family. It does MIG, TIG and laser welding, as well as conveyor systems, and takes on heavier fabrication. Visit Marpet
