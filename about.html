Our Story

Family Run Since 2006.

J.N.R. is a family run engineering firm in Hamilton, started in 2006. Between us, the team has over 100 years of experience on the tools.

When you phone, you speak to someone who works on the machines rather than a sales desk. The person pricing your job knows how long it takes to make, so the lead time you're given is one we can hit.

Our parts go into aerospace, defence, oil and gas and medical work, where a failed part isn't an option, so everything is checked against your drawing before it ships.
