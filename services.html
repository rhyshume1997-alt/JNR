05

Water jet profiling

Profiles cut from sheet and plate with a high pressure water jet. It’s a cold cut, so there’s no heat distortion and the material keeps its properties right up to the edge. Send us a DXF or a drawing and we’ll cut it to shape, ready for machining or fabrication.

  • Cold Cut
  • No Heat Distortion
  • Clean Edges
  • DXF Ready
