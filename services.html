What We Make

Milling, turning, water jet profiling, fabrication and inspection, all done in our Hamilton workshop and checked against your drawing before anything goes out.

CNC milling

Pockets, profiles and awkward contours, from a single prototype to a full production batch, held to tolerance. The 5 axis machine takes complex parts as well as straightforward ones.

Milling capacity
Machining bed1200 × 750 mm
Axes5
Tolerance±0.01 mm (most materials)
QuantitiesOne off to production

CNC turning

Shafts, flanges, bushes and fittings. We turn up to 1 metre across and 3 metres long, or up to 5 metres for slimmer parts under 160mm.

Water jet profiling

Profiles cut from sheet and plate with a high pressure water jet. It's a cold cut, so there's no heat distortion and the material keeps its properties right up to the edge.

  • Files: send a DXF or a drawing and we'll cut it to shape.
  • Next step: blanks can go straight on to machining or fabrication here.
  • Sizes and thicknesses: tell us the job and we'll confirm.

Metal fabrication

Brackets, frames, enclosures, conveyor systems and one off assemblies, welded and finished to your drawing.

  • Welding: MIG, TIG and laser
  • Materials: mild steel, stainless, aluminium

Heavier fabrication or specialist welding goes to our sister company Marpet Fabrications (opens in a new tab), run by the same family.

Inspection

Every part is measured against your drawing before it ships.

  • First article reports, supplied with the parts when the job calls for them
  • Calibrated, traceable micrometers, calipers and gauges
  • Full dimensional checks against the drawing
