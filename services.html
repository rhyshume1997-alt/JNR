Water jet profiling New

Profiles cut from sheet and plate with a high pressure water jet. It's a cold cut, so there's no heat distortion and the material keeps its properties right up to the edge.

  • Send a DXF or a drawing and we'll cut it to shape.
  • Ready for the next step. Blanks can go straight on to machining or fabrication here.
  • Sizes and thicknesses: tell us the job and we'll confirm.

Metal fabrication

Brackets, frames, enclosures, conveyor systems and one off assemblies. Mild steel, stainless or aluminium, welded and finished to your drawing.

For heavier fabrication or specialist welding, our sister company Marpet Fabrications (opens in a new tab) takes it on. It's run by the same family.

  • Welding: MIG, TIG and laser
  • Materials: mild steel, stainless, aluminium
  • Typical work: brackets, frames, enclosures, conveyor systems

Inspection
